What Webcric is

Webcric is a live cricket website that plays in the browser of a laptop, a phone or a television, with nothing to install; Android TV and Google TV can also have its app. Six channels a month, no account.

Where it plays

Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge on a laptop or phone; the Internet app on a Samsung set, Web Browser on an LG, Silk on a Fire TV. Android TV and Google TV get Webcric's own app from a six-digit code; the TV page has the steps.

Which matches

The six channels on the front page, one per fixture for the month: international series and T20 leagues. Every match page gives dates, grounds and the published start times.

A blank channel

The player is an embed from a third party; Webcric cannot tell whether a stream is on, and the box is black outside play. A channel blank during a match: email [email protected] and name the match.
